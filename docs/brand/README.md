# ShopOps brand kit

A practical starting kit for ShopOps's web browser, mobile-friendly chat client, and landing page. It translates the [selected visual direction](brand-direction.md) and [concept board](assets/shopops-brand-concept.png) into reusable brand assets. The browser and chat are separate apps.

- [Design guidelines](design-guidelines.md): mark, palette, accessibility, and application rules.
- [Voice and copy](voice-and-copy.md): English and Vietnamese product writing.
- [Landing page copy](landing-page-copy.md): proposed copy, with launch-sensitive statements flagged.
- [Brand tokens (JSON)](assets/tokens/brand-tokens.json) and [CSS](assets/tokens/brand-tokens.css): portable color, type, spacing, and radius values.
- [Logo SVGs and PNGs](assets/logos/): mark, wordmark, and lockups for light and dark backgrounds.
- [App icons and favicon](assets/app/): editable SVGs plus common web and mobile-web PNG sizes, an ICO, and an example [web manifest](assets/app/site.webmanifest).
- [Interface icons](assets/icons/): simple currentColor SVGs.
- [Social artwork](assets/social/): editable SVGs and PNG exports for open graph, square, and story formats.
- [Landing illustration](assets/landing/): editable SVG and PNG; an illustration of separate devices, not a product screenshot.

These assets interpret the selected concept and are not yet trademark-cleared or applied to either app. The SVG wordmark and social templates use text with an Inter/system fallback and are not outlined, so rendered typography can vary if Inter is unavailable. Convert to outlines with the chosen licensed font before a final identity release. The illustrative content and mockup do not prove integrations or operational capabilities.
