# Voice and product copy

ShopOps speaks like an attentive, practical teammate. Be clear, calm, and specific. Explain what happened, why it matters, and the next useful step. Be honest when evidence is incomplete, and tell the seller when approval is required.

## Writing principles

- Use familiar seller language and short, direct sentences.
- State facts as facts; label estimates and uncertainty. Never invent outcomes or user counts.
- Describe the action a control takes. Use one meaningful primary action in a decision moment.
- Avoid blame, false urgency, and promises that an agent can resolve every issue.
- Do not claim an action has completed until the system confirms it. Do not imply approval was given when it was not.

## Example product strings

| Situation | English | Vietnamese |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | Your next move, made clear. | Rõ điều cần làm, vững bước tiếp theo. |
| Empty incidents | No incidents to show yet. | Chưa có vấn đề nào cần hiển thị. |
| Attention | These orders may ship late. | Các đơn hàng này có thể giao trễ. |
| Review | Review the details | Xem chi tiết |
| Approval | Review the proposed changes before approving. | Kiểm tra thay đổi được đề xuất trước khi duyệt. |
| Uncertain evidence | I couldn't confirm the cause yet. Here's what I found. | Chưa thể xác định nguyên nhân. Đây là thông tin mình tìm được. |
| Connection error | Couldn't connect. Check your connection and try again. | Không thể kết nối. Hãy kiểm tra kết nối và thử lại. |
| Streaming interrupted | The response stopped. Check the conversation before trying again. | Phản hồi đã bị gián đoạn. Hãy kiểm tra cuộc trò chuyện trước khi thử lại. |

These are copy proposals, not assertions that the corresponding feature or data source is live. Adapt status language to the actual state returned by the product.
