# ShopOps brand direction

The selected direction is **Clarity in the chaos**. The [brand kit index](README.md) contains the logo assets, tokens, usage guidelines, and copy. The [approved concept board](assets/shopops-brand-concept.png) illustrates the direction; its product screens and conversation are not evidence of implemented features.

## Brand idea

ShopOps is an attentive operations companion for online sellers: it finds issues, explains what matters, and helps the seller decide what to do next. It should feel warm and energetic enough for commerce, yet calm and clear when a seller is dealing with a problem.

**Promise:** Know what needs attention. Know what to do next.

**Headline:** Clarity in the chaos.

**Tagline:** Your next move, made clear.

**Vietnamese direction:** Rõ điều cần làm, vững bước tiếp theo.

**Personality:** Observant, practical, calm, approachable, and respectful of the seller's control.

## Visual direction

Bring together Shopee-inspired commerce warmth and an OpenAI-inspired sense of clarity and thoughtful intelligence. This is an original ShopOps identity; it does not claim endorsement or a partnership. Do not use either company's official logo as the ShopOps mark.

The mark uses two interlocking directional shapes to suggest conversation and forward movement. Pair it with the lowercase **shopops.** wordmark. The supplied vector redraw interprets the selected concept board and provides a consistent starting asset for product and marketing surfaces. See [design guidelines](design-guidelines.md) for usage and production considerations.

### Palette

| Role | Color | Suggested use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus orange | `#F4511E` | Brand mark, selected accents, emphasis |
| Charcoal | `#1C1B1A` | Headings, primary text, strong contrast |
| Warm ivory | `#F9F6F0` | Main canvas and calm backgrounds |
| Cobalt | `#4169B2` | Informational accents and hierarchy |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, forms, and readable surfaces |

Use orange with restraint for the most important emphasis or selection. Do not rely on color alone to communicate a status; pair it with text or an icon. Check accessible contrast for text, links, controls, and semantic states.

## Type and composition

Use Inter where available with a system sans-serif fallback supporting Vietnamese. Favor clear hierarchy, readable body text, simple spacing, subtle dividers, and generous whitespace. Use bold editorial typography in marketing and maintain a calm, scannable density in product UI. Avoid generic robot imagery, neon AI effects, visual clutter, and decorative gradients.

## Applications

- **Browser app:** A standalone seller workspace with approachable commerce cues, quiet navigation, and clear separation between evidence, recommendations, and actions.
- **Mobile chat client:** A separate, mobile-friendly Telegram-style app with familiar message bubbles, a clear composer, and the ShopOps agent identity. Chat and browser are independent clients, even when displayed next to each other.
- **Notifications:** Explain what happened, why it matters, and the next step. Make approval requirements explicit; do not imply an action happened before it is confirmed.
- **Marketing and presentations:** Use the mark, ivory canvas, orange highlights, charcoal type, and concise seller-focused messages consistently.

## Voice

Write like a capable teammate: short, specific, useful, and honest about uncertainty. Lead with the seller's situation and give a clear next step. Prefer concrete evidence over alarmist language.
